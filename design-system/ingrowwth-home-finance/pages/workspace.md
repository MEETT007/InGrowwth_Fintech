# Home-finance workspace override

The master banking palette and accessible typography apply. This product is an authenticated borrower and operations workspace, so use a sidebar, task summaries, ordered case stages, and a repayment laboratory instead of a marketing hero/funnel. Keep charts subordinate to the numeric interest and principal comparison. Use navy for hierarchy, teal for the planned balance, gold sparingly for brand detail, and labelled status text alongside colour.

Density 6; motion 2. No decorative entrance animation or hover scaling. At narrow widths the navigation becomes a labelled toggle, cards stack, and only tables scroll horizontally. Controls have at least 44px targets; keyboard focus remains visible; errors are announced and input is retained. Native HTML dialogs contain application entry. The current interface supplies a light theme; do not claim a tested dark theme.
